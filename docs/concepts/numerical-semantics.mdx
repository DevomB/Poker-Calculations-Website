# Numerical semantics

The conventions every function shares: how pots are counted, how ties split, how randomness is seeded, and how hands are ordered in 169-entry arrays. The package's [`NUMERICAL.md`](https://github.com/DevomB/Poker-Calculations/blob/main/NUMERICAL.md) has the same rules alongside the source.

## Pot convention (chip math)

- `pot` / `potBeforeCall` is everything in the middle when hero acts, **villain's bet included**.
- [`expectedValueCall`](/docs/reference/api/pot-odds-and-ev/expected-value-call#expectedValueCall)`(e, pot, toCall) = e × pot − (1 − e) × toCall`, which is zero exactly at [`breakevenCallEquity`](/docs/reference/api/pot-odds-and-ev/pot-odds#breakevenCallEquity)`(pot, toCall) = toCall / (pot + toCall)`.
- The pot after a heads-up call is `pot + toCall`; after hero bets `b` and is called it is `pot + 2b`. [`sprAfterCall`](/docs/reference/api/stacks-and-sizing/spr#sprAfterCall) and [`potSizeAfterHuCall`](/docs/reference/api/pot-odds-and-ev/pot-size#potSizeAfterHuCall) follow the same rule.
- Rake variants take rake from that final pot and reduce to their unraked version at zero rake.
- Multi-street pure bluffs: a fold on street *k* wins the pot plus villain's earlier calls; being called down loses the sum of hero's bets.

## Kinds of result

Results fall into five kinds, and reference pages say which one applies where it matters:

| Kind | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact calculation | Closed form or full enumeration; the only error is floating-point rounding | [`exactHuEquityVsRange`](/docs/reference/api/exact-equity/exact-hu-vs-range#exactHuEquityVsRange), [`breakevenCallEquity`](/docs/reference/api/pot-odds-and-ev/pot-odds#breakevenCallEquity), [`monteCarloTrialsForWilsonHalfWidth`](/docs/reference/api/statistics/monte-carlo-error#monteCarloTrialsForWilsonHalfWidth) |
| Monte Carlo estimate | Sampled; repeatable for a seed, with sampling error that shrinks as `1/√trials` | [`simulateHandOutcomeDetailed`](/docs/reference/api/monte-carlo/simulate-hand-outcome#simulateHandOutcomeDetailed), [`buildPreflopEquityMatrix`](/docs/reference/api/monte-carlo/preflop-equity-matrix#buildPreflopEquityMatrix) |
| Numerical approximation | Iterative or finite-difference result of a well-defined quantity | [`icmHarvilleStackJacobian`](/docs/reference/api/icm/stack-sensitivity#icmHarvilleStackJacobian), [`cfrRiverBetCallFoldSolve`](/docs/reference/api/solvers/river-cfr#cfrRiverBetCallFoldSolve) |
| Simplified tournament model | Exact within a stated model that real tournaments only approximate | Harville ICM, [`tournamentDuelAbsorptionProbabilities`](/docs/reference/api/icm/tournament-duel#tournamentDuelAbsorptionProbabilities), the PKO knockout matrix |
| Strategy heuristic | A rule of thumb or scoring rule, not a solution | [`ruleOfTwoEquity`](/docs/reference/api/draws-and-outs/rule-of-two-and-four#ruleOfTwoEquity), [`decideAction`](/docs/reference/api/bots/decide-action#decideAction) |

## Integer arguments

Counts, indices, and sizes must be integers in the 32-bit signed range in every native export. Before 4.2.0 they were truncated or wrapped (1.9 became 1, `NaN` became 0, and 4294967297 became 1); they now throw. Seeds are the exception: they keep their documented modulo-2^32 conversion.

## Showdown equity

| Mode | Hero share when tied for best |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-way Monte Carlo (`villains` > 1) | `1 / tiedAtBest` |
| Heads-up, exact or Monte Carlo | Win `1`, chop `0.5`, loss `0` |

## Board lengths for exact equity

- [`exactHuEquityVsRandomHand`](/docs/reference/api/exact-equity/exact-hu-vs-random-hand#exactHuEquityVsRandomHand) takes an empty board (preflop) or 3–5 cards; 1–2 cards throw.
- [`exactHuEquityVsKnownHand`](/docs/reference/api/exact-equity/exact-hu-vs-known-hand#exactHuEquityVsKnownHand) and [`exactHuEquityVsRange`](/docs/reference/api/exact-equity/exact-hu-vs-range#exactHuEquityVsRange) take any board of 0–5 cards and enumerate the rest.

| Board | Villain | Work (heads-up vs a random hand) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 (preflop) | random 2 cards | C(50,2) × C(48,5) runouts: exact, but seconds |
| 3 (flop) | random 2 cards | C(47,2) × C(45,2) runouts |
| 5 (river) | random 2 cards | C(45,2) |

`exactHuEquityVsRange` costs O(|range| × runouts per combo); sparse ranges skip zero-weight combos.

## Random numbers

- Monte Carlo uses `std::mt19937` seeded with your `seed`. The same seed gives the same result on the same platform.
- Digits can differ between platforms for the same seed, because C++ standard libraries implement shuffling differently. Differences are within the Monte Carlo error.
- [`parallelHandSimulation`](/docs/reference/api/monte-carlo/parallel-hand-simulation#parallelHandSimulation): worker `t` uses seed `baseSeed + t × 9743`.

## Hand-class order (169 and 81)

Arrays with one entry per preflop hand class (Nash charts, [`buildPreflopEquityMatrix`](/docs/reference/api/monte-carlo/preflop-equity-matrix#buildPreflopEquityMatrix)) group hands by their lower card, from deuce up: the pair first, then each higher card suited and offsuit. The order starts `22, 32s, 32o, 42s, 42o, …, A2s, A2o, 33, 43s, …` and ends `KK, AKs, AKo, AA`. Index 0 is `22` and index 168 is `AA`. Short-deck arrays use the same order over ranks 6–A (81 classes, `66` to `AA`). The [preflop matrix guide](/docs/guides/preflop-equity-matrix#finding-a-hand) has a helper that builds the list.

## Preflop equity matrix

A row-major `169 × 169` matrix filled by Monte Carlo, with one representative combo per class and the two hands dealt disjoint suits in each cell. For `i ≠ j`, `M[j][i] = 1 − M[i][j]`. It is computed on the calling thread; the `threads` option is ignored and deprecated.

## Detailed Monte Carlo intervals

Each trial pays hero a share in [0, 1]: 1 for a win, `1/k` for a k-way chop, 0 for a loss. Because chops make the payoff fractional, [`simulateHandOutcomeDetailed`](/docs/reference/api/monte-carlo/simulate-hand-outcome#simulateHandOutcomeDetailed) and [`simulateEquityVsRanges`](/docs/reference/api/monte-carlo/simulate-equity-vs-range#simulateEquityVsRanges) do not treat trials as win/lose coin flips:

- `se` is `sqrt(s² / n)` with `s²` the sample variance of the payoffs. A single trial has no sample variance, so `se` is 0.5, the largest standard deviation a [0, 1] payoff can have.
- `ciLow` / `ciHigh` is the 95% Wilson score interval at the mean, `(x̄ + z²/2n ± z √(x̄(1 − x̄)/n + z²/4n²)) / (1 + z²/n)` with `z = 1.96`. A [0, 1] payoff with mean μ has variance at most μ(1 − μ), so the interval is conservative when chops are common. It does not shrink to zero when every sample is the same (a board everyone plays gives `se = 0`), because a sample can miss rare outcomes.
- Both are large-sample approximations of Monte Carlo error only.

[`monteCarloStandardError`](/docs/reference/api/statistics/monte-carlo-error#monteCarloStandardError) remains the coin-flip formula `sqrt(p (1 − p) / n)`.

## Range weights

Range weights are relative: scaling every weight by the same factor describes the same range, and every exact and Monte Carlo function gives the same result. Weights must be finite and non-negative. See [Sparse range input](/docs/concepts/sparse-range-input).

## ICM

- [`icmExpectedPayouts`](/docs/reference/api/icm/icm-expected-payouts#icmExpectedPayouts) is Harville, a simplified tournament model: each place goes to a remaining player with probability proportional to their stack among the players still unplaced. First place is therefore each stack's chip share.
- Placements are accumulated over subsets of already-placed players, not finishing orders, and payouts only resolve the places paying more than last place: `EV_i = last + Σ_{j<K} P(i at j) × (pay_j − last)`. Every place is supported for up to 20 players; larger fields work when the paid places need at most 2^20 subset states, and bigger requests throw.
- Each step's shares are scaled over the players still unplaced, so huge or tiny stacks keep their proportions: `([1e308, 1e-308, 1e-308], [100, 50, 0])` gives `[100, 25, 25]`.
- [`icmHarvilleStackJacobian`](/docs/reference/api/icm/stack-sensitivity#icmHarvilleStackJacobian) uses central differences with a step of about 6e-6 × each stack. Its columns sum to 0, and each row satisfies `Σ_j s_j × ∂EV_i/∂s_j = 0`.
- [`icmExpectedPayoutsWeitzman`](/docs/reference/api/icm/icm-expected-payouts#icmExpectedPayoutsWeitzman) is deprecated and is not a payout model: it splits the whole pool by `stack^alpha` and ignores finishing positions (`([1, 3], [100, 100])` gives `[20, 180]`, although both players are guaranteed 100).
- `icmExpectedPayouts` requires positive stacks. The helpers that value the result of a hand (blind posting, dead pots, PKO and Nash push/fold terminals) treat a seat left with zero chips as already busted: it takes the bottom prizes, and everyone else plays for the rest.

## Bounties

[`pkoKnockoutProbabilityMatrix`](/docs/reference/api/bounties/knockout-probabilities#pkoKnockoutProbabilityMatrix)`[i][j] = P(j busts) × wᵢ / Σ w` with `P(j busts) = 1 − stackⱼ / total` and `wᵢ = stackᵢ² / (stackᵢ + stackⱼ)` over the players who cover `j` (everyone else when nobody does). Columns sum to `P(j busts)`, so every bounty except the eventual winner's own is paid out.

## Tournament duel

[`tournamentDuelAbsorptionProbabilities`](/docs/reference/api/icm/tournament-duel#tournamentDuelAbsorptionProbabilities) is gambler's ruin with `h = ⌈heroStack / chipsPerAllIn⌉` and `v = ⌈villainStack / chipsPerAllIn⌉` steps: `P(hero) = (1 − rʰ) / (1 − rʰ⁺ᵛ)` with `r = (1 − p) / p`, or `h / (h + v)` at `p = 0.5`. Expected all-ins are `(h − (h + v) P) / (1 − 2p)`, or `h × v` at `p = 0.5`. `rʰ⁺ᵛ` overflows in long, uneven duels, so the formula is evaluated with `log1p` / `expm1`: 1000 vs 1000 one-chip all-ins at `p = 0.1` gives `P` ≈ 0 and 1250 expected all-ins.

## Normalization at extreme scales

[`normalizedStackFractions`](/docs/reference/api/stacks-and-sizing/effective-stack#normalizedStackFractions), the ICM family, and the other helpers that normalize weights (mystery bounties, regret matching, beta-binomial fold posteriors, Weitzman's power utilities) scale before summing. `[1e308, 1e308]` gives equal shares instead of zeros.

## Cooperative cancellation

All 14 `*Async` exports accept [`AsyncOptions`](/docs/reference/types/async-options) with `signal?: AbortSignal`. Native loops check the signal about every **4096** iterations; an abort rejects the promise with `AbortError`.

## Floating point

Most functions return `double`. Batch Monte Carlo paths may use `float` internally and widen the result. Prefer the exact functions when a spot is small enough to enumerate.
