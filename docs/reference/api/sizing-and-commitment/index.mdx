# Sizing & commitment

Bet sizing as a **fraction of pot**, **SPR after a call**, multi-street planning, and EV grids over candidate sizes. Assumes heads-up single-call geometry for `sprAfterCall`.

Pair with [Pot & EV](/docs/reference/api/pot-and-ev/) for breakeven equity and with [Stacks & display](/docs/reference/api/stacks-and-display/) for pre-call SPR.
